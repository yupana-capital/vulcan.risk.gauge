About the Vulcan Risk Gauge

The Vulcan Risk Gauge is a daily reading of market risk for US growth equities, as tracked by the Nasdaq-100 (QQQ). It is updated daily at 9:30pm PT.

The level

  • 1-2 goldilocks
  • 3-4 confident
  • 5 mixed
  • 6-7 cautious
  • 8-10 defensive

The arrow

  • ↑ risk has been rising over the past week
  • → risk is broadly steady
  • ↓ risk has been easing

Signal Strength

Signal Strength is the probability that today's reading will be the same a week from today.

Source

The gauge comes from a proprietary multi-asset trend and volatility model.

Disclaimer

This is not a forecast or a recommendation. A high reading does not mean a fall is certain, and a low reading does not mean markets will go parabolic.
